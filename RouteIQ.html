Data and settings

Your data stays on this phone.
Nothing is uploaded. Trips are stored in this browser. Clearing browser data deletes them, so export a backup now and then.
What this app can and can't do
Records trips with GPS while the screen is on and this page is open. Phones pause web pages in the background or when the screen locks, so keep RouteIQ in front.

It has no maps or turn-by-turn directions of its own. "Navigate" hands the place to Google Maps (or another app you choose). If you downloaded offline maps in Google Maps, that app can use them.

Distances are estimates. Poor GPS signal can shift them by a few percent, and points with accuracy worse than 40 m are skipped.
